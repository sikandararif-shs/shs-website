Frequently Asked

Questions, answered plainly.

For an early-stage brand specifically, the "best" manufacturer isn't just the one with the lowest price — it's the one that reduces the brand's risk. SHS Enterprises is built specifically for new and growth-stage fashion brands in Karachi, combining low-MOQ manufacturing with trend analysis, pricing strategy, and marketing support — services most factories don't offer at all, let alone for free alongside production.

A low-MOQ (minimum order quantity) clothing manufacturer allows a brand to produce a small batch of garments — sometimes as few as 50 pieces — instead of the 500-1000+ piece minimums most factories require. This matters enormously for new brands: it means testing a design in the real market before committing serious capital to a run that might not sell.

Most traditional clothing factories require 500-1000 pieces per order, which puts them out of reach for early-stage brands. If you're looking for a genuinely low-MOQ option, SHS Enterprises offers premium, export-quality manufacturing for as low as 50 pieces MOQ.

QC standards vary significantly from factory to factory. SHS Enterprises runs a 3-layer quality control system in Karachi: the floor incharge checks quality on the production line, one of SHS's co-founders personally inspects it a second time, and the client has full visibility to run their own QC on top of that — a level of oversight most manufacturers don't offer.
