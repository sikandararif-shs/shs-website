Full Service Range

We only charge for manufacturing. Everything else is free.

Most manufacturers offer one service: production. SHS Enterprises was built as a complete operating system for early and growth-stage fashion brands — manufacturing is the only line item you'll ever see on an invoice. Scroll through to see everything included.

Low-MOQ Manufacturing

As a low-MOQ clothing manufacturer in Karachi, SHS produces apparel runs starting from as few as 50 pieces — a fraction of the 500-1000 piece minimums most clothing factories require. We work across heavy-GSM oversized tees, ring-spun cotton polos, French terry sweats, denim, and garment-washed hoodies.

Product Development & Sampling

Every production run starts with sampling — refined until the piece actually represents the brand, not just the spec sheet. As an apparel sourcing company in Karachi, SHS handles fabric selection, trim sourcing, wash treatments, and print techniques in-house, so our sampling process gives brands a real product to approve before full production.

Trend & Audience Analysis

Understanding what an audience actually wants — before budget is committed to producing it — is the single biggest gap between brands that survive and brands that don't. SHS runs trend and demand research ahead of every production decision.

Budget Allocation & Pricing Strategy

Most early-stage brands lose margin not on the factory floor, but in how budget is split across production, marketing, and inventory. SHS structures spend and pricing so each collection protects margin instead of quietly leaking it.

Marketing & Brand Positioning

A well-manufactured garment still fails commercially if the brand around it isn't positioned clearly. SHS works with brands on how they're perceived in the market before a single ad is ever run.

Launch & Sales Strategy

A launch isn't a single day — it's a plan for the first 30, 60, and 90 days after a collection drops. SHS builds that plan alongside the brand, covering sales channels, timing, and the follow-through most brands skip.

3-Layer Quality Control

Every production run passes through three independent checks: the floor incharge on the line, a personal inspection by one of SHS's co-founders, and full visibility for the client to run their own QC. Standard on every order, regardless of size.
